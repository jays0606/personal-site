## Why

Korean voice-phishing losses passed a trillion won in the first ten months of 2025. Victims over sixty went from 16% of cases to 31% in five years; the average loss per victim rose almost six times. The moment that matters is the thirty seconds before money moves, and the person in that moment is usually alone.

## How

Everything runs on the phone. A detector call extracts scam patterns from the call and asks for lookups; a dispatcher runs seven tools against a bundled intelligence index; a skeptic call argues the legitimate-call position with the tool results in view; a reconciler produces a calibrated risk snapshot and a structured action. If the risk is high, the bank app gets a "pause" and a chosen family member gets a check-in. Fear should not be faster than help.
