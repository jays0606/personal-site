## The pattern

Each document gets a tree: sections, summaries, page ranges. Video lectures get transcribed and cut into four-minute blocks. An agent searches the trees with BM25, reads the pages it chooses, and answers with citation tokens that the front end turns into links — to the page image, or to the exact second of the lecture. It's the PageIndex approach: the model navigates a table of contents instead of being handed embedding neighbours, so the citations are real.

## 몸톡

For a pilates studio whose faculty come from dance anatomy: six textbooks (642 pages) and seventeen Korean lecture videos (about twelve and a half hours) — 836 citable units. The system prompt takes an explicit stance against studio folklore: forced turnout from the knee, ballistic stretching, weight rules for growing dancers. The audience is anatomy and dance students, young dancers, and the teachers who were taught the folklore.

## What I learned about models

For an agent loop, a fast model that takes seventeen seconds a round beats a strong one that takes a hundred and seventy. Gemini Flash runs these; the Pro preview was unusable for conversation.
