## What I built

- A gateway over OpenAI, Gemini, and Anthropic with cost-aware routing per tenant.
- Per-tenant credit metering and billing.
- The RAG pipeline — ingestion, embedding, retrieval — with quality monitoring on the answers, not just the retrieval.
- Long-term contextual memory with adaptive recall.
- PromptOps: hallucination detection, cost and latency dashboards, prompt versioning with CI.

Deployed at Seoul National, Sogang, Chungnam National, and dozens more. It's the platform Jarvis was born next to, and the reason I trust "fifty enterprise clients" as a number.
