Mediapipe pose, hands, and face fused into one skeleton; 52 ARKit-compatible blendshapes for the face; VRM avatars in Three.js; thirty frames a second for streaming. The first real-time computer-vision product I shipped, and the reason the [face landmark demo](/work/mediapipe-facelandmark) exists.
