## The idea

Three things missing from every astrology app: a journaling loop keyed to today's transits against *your* chart, with old entries resurfacing when the same transit returns; an AI that grounds every sentence in a verifiable placement; and, later, a couples mode that tracks composite transits.

## What exists

Swiss Ephemeris (`pyswisseph`) for natal charts and transits, so the math is real. Gemini Flash with minimal thinking for the words, verified to cite real placements. FastAPI with nineteen endpoints; Postgres; Expo Router on the phone; streaming answers over SSE; city autocomplete via Open-Meteo; account deletion and a GDPR export. Apple and Google sign-in are wired on the client and wait on a developer account and a native build.

A sibling app for the Korean market — 사주, the same shape with a different engine — is at the same stage.
