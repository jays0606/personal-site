## What it does

A guesthouse owner pastes `<script src="…/widget.js" data-tenant="…">` into their site. Guests get a chat bubble that answers check-in times, parking, and the pet policy in whatever language they ask in — and when it doesn't know, it says so and collects the question for the owner instead of guessing. Owners get a dashboard with every conversation, FAQ import from a spreadsheet, a QR code for the counter, and a plain-language editor: "change the checkout time to 11" updates the FAQ.

## How it's built

- FastAPI, Tortoise ORM, pydantic-ai over Gemini Flash, streaming over SSE with three-day session reuse and rate limits.
- A platform-owned prompt frame the tenant can't break: answer only from FAQs, refuse and escalate otherwise, reply in the user's language, plain text.
- Next.js for the widget loader, the owner accounts, and the public shop pages.
- Two Cloud Run services and a small Cloud SQL instance in Seoul. The whole thing was built and verified end to end — including the widget pasted into a raw `file://` page — in a weekend.

## Who uses it

A music studio, a lifestyle shop, a friend's finance site, a research institute's support line, and a guesthouse. One signed contract so far. Kakao channel integration and custom domains are the next two asks; both are deferred until more than one customer wants them.

This is the [Jarvis](/work/jarvis) idea at its smallest: an agent that knows *this* business, living where the customers already are.
